\begin{abstract}
This report records the experimental campaign on the Plasma Fluid Finite-Difference Time-Domain (PF-FDTD) solver from the February 2026 dipole-in-plasma baseline through the September 2026 low-frequency CW sheath extension. The design target was a Tu-inspired upward shift of series resonance \(\fres\) with vacuum-sheath width \(\Sd\). Early pulse and CW campaigns either failed as measurements or showed \(Z\) independent of \(\Sd\). A coupling bug---sheath depletion seeded from the plasma-on mask before antenna geometry existed---placed the density hole at the domain edge rather than next to the wire. After a PEC-seeded fix, feed impedance depends strongly on sheath width. Dense CW at \(\fp=\SI{2}{\mega\hertz}\) plus a low-frequency extension locate \(\fres(\Sd=0)\approx\SI{1.846}{\mega\hertz}\), \(\fres(\Sd=2)\approx\SI{0.655}{\mega\hertz}\), and \(\Sd=10\) capacitive to \(\SI{0.50}{\mega\hertz}\): with the \(\ImZ\) \(+\) to \(-\) metric, \(\fres\) moves \emph{downward} with \(\Sd\). Chapter~\ref{ch:lowf} presents the spectra and resonance plots; Chapter~\ref{ch:discrepancy} states the Tu discrepancy and attributes it primarily to series sheath capacitance dominating a prescribed volumetric jacket (Balmain--Liu lineage), not to dielectric unloading toward free space.
\end{abstract}
