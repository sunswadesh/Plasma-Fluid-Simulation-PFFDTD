\chapter{Status and Next Steps}
\label{ch:status}

\section{Where the campaign stands}

The work from February through 4 September 2026 is one antenna-in-plasma story on a single dipole grid.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{February baseline.} Free-space versus plasma impedance is measurable. Same geometry as all later sheath runs.
\item \textbf{May pulse \(\Sd\) sweep.} Failed as a measurement (no usable \(\fres\); GHz FFT artifacts). Independently, the sheath was not on the wire.
\item \textbf{Low-frequency CW (pre-fix).} Measurement method works; frequencies sit below resonance; \(Z\) still independent of \(\Sd\).
\item \textbf{CW \(\fp\)-screen.} Measurement success (\(\fres\approx\SI{1.775}{\mega\hertz}\)) and physics failure (\(Z(\Sd=0)\equiv Z(\Sd=10)\) to \(\sim 0.003\%\)). Negative control, keep the folder.
\item \textbf{Coupling fix.} Sheath had been seeded from the plasma-on mask before PEC existed; depletion sat at the domain edge. PEC-seeded \texttt{ApplySheath} after \texttt{setup2} puts the hole next to the wire.
\item \textbf{Post-fix confirm.} \(\lvert\Delta Z\rvert/\lvert Z\rvert\sim 90\)--\(110\%\) at 1.7/1.9~MHz. Pulse FFT now sees \(\Sd\) but is too coarse for a precision \(\fres(\Sd)\) curve.
\item \textbf{Dense CW (August).} All 66 cases finished. \(\fres(\Sd=0)\approx\SI{1.846}{\mega\hertz}\). \(\Sd\ge 2\) capacitive in 1.50--2.30~MHz.
\item \textbf{Low-f CW (September).} 0.50--1.50~MHz for \(\Sd=0,2,10\). \(\fres(\Sd=2)\approx\SI{0.655}{\mega\hertz}\); \(\Sd=10\) capacitive down to \(\SI{0.50}{\mega\hertz}\) (Chapter~\ref{ch:lowf}).
\item \textbf{Tu discrepancy.} Upward \(\fres(\Sd)\) not reproduced; interpreted as series \(C_{\mathrm{sh}}\) domination of a prescribed jacket (Chapter~\ref{ch:discrepancy}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Combined scientific conclusion}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item PF-FDTD resolves plasma-loaded dipole impedance near \(\fp\) on this grid.
\item After the PEC-seeded fix, a prescribed volumetric sheath \emph{does} change feed \(Z\) strongly.
\item With the campaign metric (first \(\ImZ\) \(+\) to \(-\) crossing), increasing \(\Sd\) moves \(\fres\) \emph{downward} (or removes the crossing), consistent with \textbf{series} sheath-capacitance domination---not with the upward dielectric-unloading target used to design the sweeps.
\item The remaining problem is model/observable choice (volumetric gap vs analytic \(C_{\mathrm{sh}}\); phase-zero vs magnitude peak; Tu kinetic target vs prescribed jacket), not missing frequency coverage.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Open items}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Compare \(\fres(\Sd=0)\) and low-frequency crossings to free-space \(\fres\) on the same dipole grid.
\item Optional: denser CW only around \(\Sd=2\) (0.55--0.75~MHz) if a precision pick for that island is required.
\item Post-process low-\(f\) capacitive asymptotes to equivalent \(C_{\mathrm{sh}}(\Sd)\) and compare to a coax estimate.
\item Decide whether an upward Tu-style \(\fres(\Sd)\) remains the right validation target for a prescribed vacuum jacket.
\item Optional: re-run the \(\fp\)-screen with fixed physics as a published before/after pair (documentation, not debugging).
\item Software not in this report's critical path: GPU, MPI, broader unit-test coverage.
\end{enumerate}
